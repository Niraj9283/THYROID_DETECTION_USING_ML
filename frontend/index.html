      Evaluation Methodology

      • Dataset: 30,000 anonymized clinical thyroid screening records with 27 raw feature fields.
      • Train/Test Split: 80% training (24,000 samples) and 20% stratified testing (6,000 samples).
      • Ensemble Voting: Weighted soft-voting with probability calibration across individual models.

      Intended vs Non-Intended Use

      ✔ Intended Use:

      Preliminary risk stratification, patient education, reference range comparison, and identifying patients who require endocrinologist consultation.

      ✖ Non-Intended Use:

      Automated clinical diagnosis, solitary basis for surgical decisions, or initiating prescription medication without confirmatory laboratory assays.

      Known Limitations & Bias Considerations

      Cross-sectional datasets do not account for transient non-thyroidal illness (euthyroid sick syndrome), pregnancy-induced trimester hormone shifts, or lab-specific reference range differences without clinician review.
